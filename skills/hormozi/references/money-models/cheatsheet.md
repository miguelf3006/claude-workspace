# Cheatsheet — $100M Money Models

## The Four Offer Types (audit your business against this)
| Type | Job | If missing… |
|---|---|---|
| **Attraction** | Turn strangers into customers | Nothing else matters — build first |
| **Upsell** | Get more cash, faster | You're capped at one transaction per customer |
| **Downsell** | Turn noes into yeses | You lose everyone who says no |
| **Continuity** | Keep them buying | Revenue restarts from zero every month |

## The Design Target
**Cover the cost of acquiring a customer within 30 days** — so the same interest-free credit funds the next one. Value × volume × speed compound: 2× each = **8× growth**; 3× each = **27×**.

## Pick Your Play
| Situation | Play |
|---|---|
| Need leads cheap, sell premium in the room | **Decoy offer** (Ch 5) |
| Want many qualified leads fast | **Free giveaway** (Ch 4) |
| Sell something people start and quit | **Win your money back** (Ch 3) |
| Sell something buyable in quantity/duration | **Buy X get Y free** (Ch 6) |
| One-time product, provable fast result | **Pay less now or pay more later** (Ch 7) |
| Just made a sale | **Classic upsell** (Ch 8) |
| Multiple products, live conversation | **Menu upsell** (Ch 9) |
| High ticket, unknown budget | **Anchor upsell** (Ch 10) |
| Lapsed / upset / competitor's customers | **Rollover upsell** (Ch 11) |
| "I can't afford it" | **Payment plan** (Ch 12) |
| Recurring service, customer must do work | **Trial with penalty** (Ch 12) |
| High-ticket price objection, won't discount | **Feature downsell** (Ch 12) |
| Converting buyers to recurring | **Continuity bonus** (Ch 13) |
| Long-cycle service, commitment is normal | **Continuity discount** (Ch 13) |
| Want term commitments without hard selling | **Waived fee** (Ch 13) |

## Numbers Worth Memorizing
| Rule | Number |
|---|---|
| Cost recovery window | **30 days** |
| Win-your-money-back refund rate | **~10%** of customers |
| Rollover: price next offer at | **≥4×** the credit (= ≤25% discount) |
| Pay-now discount | **20–50%** + bonuses |
| Pay-later cancellation alarm threshold | **>10%** |
| Giveaway discount | **10–30%** of gross margins |
| Waived setup fee | **3–5×** the monthly rate |
| Weekly vs. monthly billing | **13 cycles vs. 12 = +8.3%/yr** (at 20% margins, **+41% profit**) |
| Processing fee | **3%** — at 10% margins, **+30% profit** |
| Bulk prepaid ("buy 5 get 1") | **1 in 8** takers = **+50%** 30-day profit |
| Exit interviews | save **~1/3** of cancellers |

## Standalone Price Dial (Ch 13)
Want more people on continuity? Raise the standalone price relative to it.
| % choosing continuity | Standalone priced at |
|---|---|
| 50% | 1.33× |
| 70% | 2× |
| 90% | 2.66× |
*Corollary: people pay ~33% more just to avoid continuity.*

## Never Ask Yes/No — Ask Which
| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| "Do you want the protein?" | "Chocolate or vanilla?" |
| "Do you want to pay?" | "Want to just use the card on file?" |
| "Do you want to start?" | "Start today or Monday?" |
| "Anything else?" | "You don't want to store anything else, do you?" |
| "Want a discount?" | "Keep the guarantee, or pay less without it?" |

## Downselling Rules
- They said no to **this** offer, not all offers
- Downsells are **trades** — if you give something, get something
- **Personalize, don't pressure**
- **Offer something different for less. Never the same thing for less.**
- Descending prices destroy trust: $5,000 → $400 lost the insurance sale *and* the car sale

## Mirror Pairs (build one, get two)
- **Win your money back** ↔ **Trial with penalty** — refund if they comply / charge if they don't. Same criteria, opposite incentive.
- **Buy X get Y free** ↔ **Continuity discount** — one-time vs. recurring.
- **Decoy offer** ↔ **Anchor upsell** — cheap-first vs. expensive-first contrast.

## Tells & Smells
- Giveaway got no interest → **the prize wasn't grand enough**
- Decoy isn't converting to premium → **contrast too small**
- Too many taking pay-later → **discount pay-now more, add bonuses**
- Nobody takes the trial → **lower requirements or penalties**
- Trial takers don't follow through → **explain how fees help; make sales meetings mandatory**
- Trial takers don't stay → **front end doesn't match back end**
- Lots of customers buying small → **bump to medium.** Lots buying large → **raise all prices**
- Tempted to drop the price → **cut a feature instead**
