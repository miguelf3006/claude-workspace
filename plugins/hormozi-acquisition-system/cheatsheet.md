## The Master Diagnostic (walk top-down, stop at first no)
1. **Market** — pain · purchasing power · targetability · growth? *(any failure is disqualifying)*
2. **Offer** — can they price-compare you? *(if they ask "how are you different," it failed)*
3. **Volume** — 100 primary actions/day? *(if not, you have no data)*
4. **CAC vs. 3× industry average** — below: fix the model · above: fix the ads
5. **Qualified and buying?** — qualified-not-buying = **sales problem**
6. **30-day recovery?** — no = money model problem

## The Mirror Pairs (build one, get two)
- **Win your money back** ↔ **Trial with penalty** — refund if they comply / charge if they don't
- **Buy X get Y free** ↔ **Continuity discount** — one-time / recurring
- **Decoy offer** ↔ **Anchor upsell** — cheap-first / expensive-first contrast
- **Conditional guarantee** ↔ **Feature downsell** — add the guarantee / price its removal

## Sequencing Rules Across the System
- **Build**: offer → leads → money model
- **Channels**: warm outreach → content → cold outreach → paid ads
- **Lead getters**: customers → employees → agencies → affiliates
- **Money model**: attraction → upsell → downsell → continuity
- **Amplify**: below $1M profit *more, better, new* · above *better, more, new*
- **Money**: create flow → monetize flow → add friction

## Wrong Self-Diagnoses (and what they actually are)
| They say | It usually is |
|---|---|
| "I've saturated my market" | Size of the pie fallacy — one platform, no content, no outreach |
| "My ads don't work" | A sales problem, if qualified leads are taking calls |
| "My CAC is too high" | Low LTGP — competitors' CACs are closer than you think |
| "I need more leads" | An offer problem wearing a costume |
| "Everyone loves our stuff" | An unremarkable product — you'd have referrals if they did |
| "This won't work for my business" | *"How will I make this work for my business?"* |
| "I'm capped at $3M" | *"You just need to be better"* |

## Universal Rules
- Never discount the **core** offer — add bonuses. Discount only at the **entry**.
- Offer something different for less. **Never the same thing for less.**
- Every guarantee needs the "or what," and its conditions should be the success behaviours.
- Ask **which**, never **whether**.
- Ask **who**, not **if**, for referrals — at the moment of purchase.
- Real scarcity and real deadlines only. Credibility is what makes the stack work.
- Refund anyone who asks. Don't take blood money.
- Depersonalize failure: your offer sucks, not you.
