# Glossary — The Hormozi Acquisition System

*Cross-book terms only. For terms specific to one book, see that skill's glossary.*

**Acquisition equation** — Growth = offer strength × volume of engaged leads × speed of cash recovery. Multiplicative; a zero anywhere is a zero everywhere. (Ch 1)

**Amplification order** — Below $1M/yr profit: more → better → new. Above: better → more → new. (Ch 7)

**Build order** — Offer → leads → money model. Causal, not arbitrary. (Ch 1)

**CAC** — Cost to acquire a customer. Lowered by *Leads*. (Ch 4)

**Constraint** — The step where the most is lost; where the smallest improvement produces the biggest gain. Both a funnel concept (*Leads*) and a business-level one (Ch 2). (Ch 2, Ch 7)

**Constraint chain** — Each book solves the problem the previous one exposes: not enough cash → not enough clients → cash arrives too slowly. (Ch 1)

**Enhancer stack** — Scarcity, urgency, bonuses, guarantees, applied to the offer, the lead magnet, and the money model. (Ch 5)

**Guarantee lifecycle** — Conversion tool → results mechanism → attraction offer → downsell → priceable feature. (Ch 5)

**LTGP** — Lifetime gross profit: all money a customer spends minus all money to deliver. Never revenue. (Ch 4)

**LTGP:CAC** — The shared scoreboard across all three books. Target > 3:1. (Ch 4)

**Master diagnostic** — Market → offer → lead volume → ads vs. model → ads vs. sales → cash speed. Walk top-down, stop at the first failure. (Ch 2)

**Mirror pairs** — Plays that are the same structure with one variable flipped: win-your-money-back ↔ trial-with-penalty; buy-X-get-Y-free ↔ continuity discount; decoy ↔ anchor. (Ch 5)

**Order of importance** — Market > offer > persuasion. A great rating higher up overpowers everything below; a bad one stops the equation. (Ch 2)

**Rule of 100** — 100 primary actions per day for 100 days. The constant across every channel. (Ch 6)

**Size of the pie fallacy** — Mistaking the slice you advertise into for the entire market. The most expensive self-diagnosis in the system. (Ch 2)

**Three numbers** — LTGP, CAC, 30-day cash. Each book moves a different one. (Ch 4)

**30-day recovery** — Recovering acquisition cost within the interest-free credit window so the same money funds the next customer. (Ch 4)

**Two big kahunas** — Not enough clients, not enough cash. Every business problem reduces to one of these. (Ch 1)

**Value equation** — (Dream outcome × perceived likelihood) ÷ (time delay × effort and sacrifice). Runs all three books, not just *Offers*. (Ch 3)
