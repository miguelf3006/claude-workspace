# Chapter 6: The Zero-to-First-$100k Playbook

## Core Idea
A single ordered sequence, drawn from all three books, for someone starting with **no customers, no audience, no list, and no ad budget.** Every step is chosen because it requires only what the previous step produced.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The Zero-to-$100k Sequence**:

| Phase | Move | Source | Why here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Market** | Score pain, purchasing power, targetability, growth. Pick a narrow avatar and **commit** | *Offers* ch4 | A bad market cannot be out-executed |
| **1. Offer** | Dream outcome → problem list → solutions → delivery vehicles → trim and stack | *Offers* ch8–9 | Traffic amplifies whatever offer sits beneath it |
| **2. Name it** | MAGIC: 3–5 of magnet, avatar, goal, interval, container | *Offers* ch15 | An unnamed offer converts badly regardless of quality |
| **3. First five customers** | Warm outreach. List every contact. 100/day. ACA. *"Do you know anybody…"* Make the **first five free** | *Leads* ch5 | Costs nothing, works with zero audience, generates reps and testimonials |
| **4. Start charging** | The moment referrals begin: 80% off for the next five, then 60%, then 40%, raising 20% every five | *Leads* ch5 | Referrals are the litmus test that you're good enough to charge |
| **5. Guarantee** | Offer it only to prepayers. *"Would you rather pay less today, or get all your money back?"* | *Leads* ch5 / *Offers* ch14 | Collects cash upfront and filters for committed clients |
| **6. Lead magnet** | Narrow problem → three types × four vehicles → **test the name** → package every format | *Leads* ch3 | Makes every subsequent channel cheaper |
| **7. Content** | Hook/retain/reward. 100 minutes a day, ≥1 post a day. **Give until they ask** | *Leads* ch6–7 | Compounds an audience while costing only time |
| **8. First money-model layer** | Add a **classic upsell** at point of sale, then a **payment plan downsell** | *Money Models* ch8, ch12 | Doubles cash per conversation with no new traffic |
| **9. Referrals** | Ask **at the point of purchase**, three-way intro, ask *who* not *if* | *Leads* ch12 | Adds ~25–30% more signups at zero cost |
| **10. Cold outreach** | Build the list, personalize, **big fast value**, 100/day, expect ~a year to scale | *Leads* ch8 | Only now — it takes volume and patience you didn't have at step 3 |
| **11. Paid ads** | Track → lose → print. Test at 2× 30-day cash | *Leads* ch9–10 | Last, because it needs both skill and money the earlier steps produced |

- **The Rule of 100 as the constant**: 100 primary actions per day for 100 days, whichever channel you're on (*Leads* ch11).
- **The milestone that matters**: $100,000 **in your personal account** — not revenue, not profit in the business account, not money earmarked for debt (*Offers* ch16).

## Key Concepts
- **Why free first**: You get reps making offers, people are forgiving when they haven't paid, and you need guinea pigs to work the kinks out. Free customers convert, refer, and produce testimonials (*Leads* ch5).
- **Learning, not earning**: *"The objective right now is to learn, not to earn."* Your stuff isn't valuable yet.
- **Create flow, monetize flow, then add friction** (*Offers* ch9): overdeliver like crazy at first, then use the cash flow to fix operations.
- **Hidden costs diagnose the free-offer failure**: If you can't give it away, either they don't want it (outcome), don't believe you (likelihood), or the time/effort is too high. *"Your free stuff is too expensive."*

## Mental Models
- **Skill ladder, not channel preference.** Warm outreach needs nothing. Content needs skill. Cold outreach needs volume and systems. Paid ads need money *and* skill. Climb in order.
- **Every step funds the next.** This is why the order isn't negotiable for someone starting at zero.
- **Depersonalize every no.** *"Yes gives me opportunity. No gives me feedback. Either way, I win."*

## Anti-patterns
- **Starting with paid ads**: The most expensive way to learn what a working offer feels like.
- **Perfecting an untested product**: Eight Sundays on a webinar nobody watched vs. 13 minutes on a screen recording that filled a calendar (*Leads* ch2).
- **Never raising prices**: Free forever means you never learn whether you're good.
- **Undersized tests**: 300 flyers taught nothing. 0.5% is decent, 1% is a winner — so test at 5,000 (*Leads* ch16).

## Worked Example
**Hormozi's own zero-to-first-business, mapped to the sequence.** He quit his job, set up an LLC (named *Impetus Group* until the first person he told said *"Impotence?"*), and then — without knowing it was called warm outreach:

> *"Hey, do you know anyone who's trying to get into shape? I'm training people for free for 12 weeks. On top of that, I'll make them a custom nutrition plan and grocery list. All they have to do is donate to a charity of their choice and let me use their testimonial."*

**Six people said yes** — two high-school friends, one college friend, three referrals. After the 12-week charity period he asked them to pay *him*. Nobody minded. He asked for referrals: **five or six more**, all paying directly. That business made **~$4,000/month** and replaced his job income.

Total infrastructure: a tax ID, a bank account, a way to take payments, and a way to communicate with people. *"That last part is the most important. It's how you get leads."*

Note which steps he skipped and what it cost him: no offer work (so he later couldn't sell $99 boot camps against a $29 competitor), no lead magnet, no money model. Each of those became a later book.

**The first-90-days version**, if you want a concrete plan:
- **Days 1–7**: market scoring, offer build, MAGIC name
- **Days 8–37**: 100 warm reach-outs/day; first five free; collect testimonials
- **Days 30–60**: start charging, raising 20% every five customers; add a classic upsell and payment plan
- **Days 30–90**: 100 minutes/day of content, ≥1 post/day, give until they ask
- **Days 60–90**: build and test the lead magnet; ask every buyer for a referral at purchase
- **Day 90+**: only now consider cold outreach or paid ads

## Key Takeaways
1. Market → offer → name → warm outreach. Nothing before this needs money.
2. First five free, then raise 20% every five once referrals start.
3. Add one upsell and one downsell before adding any new traffic source.
4. Ask for referrals at the moment of purchase, every time.
5. Rule of 100 on whichever channel you're on. 100 days minimum.
6. Cold outreach and paid ads come last, funded by what the earlier steps produced.
7. Measure the milestone in your personal account, not the P&L.
