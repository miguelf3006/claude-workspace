# Cheatsheet — $100M Leads

## The Core Four (locate every tactic here)
| | One-to-one (private) | One-to-many (public) |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm** (gave permission) | Warm outreach | Post free content |
| **Cold** (didn't) | Cold outreach | Paid ads |
*Not enough leads = not enough **skill** or not enough **volume**. Diagnose which; they have different fixes.*

## Diagnostic Decision Tree
1. **Are you getting engaged leads at all?** No → are you doing 100 primary actions/day? If not, that's the answer.
2. **CAC vs. industry average**: below 3× → fix the **business model** (raise LTGP). Above 3× → fix **advertising**.
3. **Do engaged leads have the problem you solve *and* the money?**
   - No → not qualified → **advertising problem**
   - Yes, buying, but too few → **advertising problem**
   - Yes, qualified, not buying → **sales problem** *(don't fire the ads team)*
4. **Referrals vs. churn**: referrals > churn → you can grow with no advertising. referrals < churn → you're advertising to stand still.

## Thresholds & Benchmarks
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| **LTGP:CAC** | **> 3:1** (below this, businesses don't scale) |
| Cold email → engaged leads | **~3% of list** (30% open × 10% reply) |
| Cold call | 100 calls → 20% pickup → 25% take the magnet = **4 engaged leads** (~1/hour) |
| Cold DM / voice memo | 100 personalized → **20% reply** |
| Ad test budget | **2× 30-day cash collected**; kill at 1× if zero leads |
| Budget scaling pad | **+20%** (ads lose efficiency as they scale) |
| Give:ask ratio | ≥ 3.5:1; growing platforms over-give far more |
| Rule of 100 | **100 actions/day for 100 days** |
| Referral ask at signup | expect **+25–30%** signups |
| Flyer response | 0.5% decent, 1% winner → **test at 5,000, not 300** |
| Employee trial | **3 weeks**, then cut |
| Affiliate certification price | **10–20%** of average affiliate's first-year earnings |
| Creative volume | top 0.1% of advertisers test **11× more** creative |

## Where to Spend Effort
- **First 5 seconds of an ad** → Hormozi's advertising became **20× more effective** when he focused there.
- **The constraint** (biggest drop-off step) → +5 points on a 5% step doubles leads; +5 on a 50% step adds 10%.
- **Front of the funnel** usually is the constraint (1% click, then 30% opt in).
- **Headline** → "you've spent 80 cents of your advertising dollar" (Ogilvy).

## Testing Discipline
- **One test per week per platform.** Never more.
- **Log every result** — start "a zillion improvements later, not at square one."
- **Give up after 4 tries or 1 month**, then move to the next constraint.
- Don't waste a weekly test on red vs. bright red.

## Sequencing Rules
- **Channel order**: warm outreach → free content → cold outreach → paid ads. Skills and cash from the earlier ones fund the later ones.
- **Amplification order**: below $1M profit → **more, better, new**. Above → **better, more, new**.
- **Lead getter order**: customers (referrals) → employees → agencies → affiliates.
- **The loop**: run a daily action until you can pay someone to do it → then advertise for *employees* → repeat.

## Tells & Smells
- *"I've saturated my market"* → almost always the **size of the pie fallacy**. Check platforms used, content produced, outreach done.
- *"Everyone loves our stuff, we just need to get the word out"* → the product isn't remarkable. If it were, you'd have more business than you could handle.
- *"Cold outreach doesn't work"* after 12 weeks → veterans say a year. Check metrics tracking first.
- *"I reached out to 100 people over six weeks"* → that's 1/42nd of the work. It's 100 **per day**.
- *"Advertising doesn't work"* while qualified leads take calls → sales problem.
- Ads suddenly stopped working → check whether you stopped making content for that audience.

## Scripts Worth Memorizing
- **Warm ask**: "Do you know anybody who is [struggle] looking to [dream outcome] in [time]? I'm taking on five case studies for free…" → *"Does anyone you like come to mind?"*
- **Nine-word email**: "Are you still looking to [four-word desire]?"
- **Referral at purchase**: "People who do our program with someone else tend to get 3× the results. **Who** else could you do this with?"
- **Agency terms**: "I'd like to work with you for 6 months so I can learn how you do it — and I'll pay extra for you to break down *why* you make the decisions you do. Are you opposed to this?"
- **Prepay + guarantee**: "Would you rather pay less today, or get all your money back?"
