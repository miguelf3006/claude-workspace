## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### A Money Model Is a Sequence of Offers
Find every opportunity to solve a customer's problem, then offer to solve it. *"If you offer the right thing when customers realize they need it, you can make as many offers as you like."*

**The design target: recover the cost of acquiring a customer within 30 days.** Why 30 specifically — any business can get interest-free money for 30 days on a credit card. Get a customer, get paid back, clear the balance, do it again.

**The compounding:** make customers 2× as valuable, get 2× as many, at 2× the speed → the business grows **8× faster.** Triple all three → **27×.**

### The Four Offer Types
| Type | Job |
|---|---|
| **Attraction** | Turn strangers into customers |
| **Upsell** | Get people to spend more cash |
| **Downsell** | Get people to say yes when they'd have said no |
| **Continuity** | Keep people buying |

Build in that order — cash is the constraint on everything upstream. Missing types are your build list.

### The Five Attraction Offers
- **Win your money back** — they pay, then earn it back (cash or store credit) by hitting **results**, taking **actions**, or **both**. Make the conditions the behaviours that cause success. Plan for ~10% asking for money back, and **have an upsell ready for winners' credit.**
- **Free giveaway** — one grand prize with a stated value that anchors; **everyone else qualifies for a discounted version.** Discount = 10–30% of gross margins. Urgency at three points: enter, claim, use.
- **Decoy offer** — advertise the stripped version (no guarantee, fewer components), present both in the room, emphasize premium. *"Are you here for free stuff or lasting results?"*
- **Buy X get Y free** — reframe a discount as free stuff. Three for $30 becomes *one for $30, get two free.* **Always give more free items than paid.** Raise prices permanently first.
- **Pay less now or pay more later** — pay-later ($0 today, conditional guarantee) offered first; then pay-now at 20–50% off plus bonuses.

### The Four Upsells
- **Classic** — solve the next problem your own offer just revealed, the moment it appears. *"You can't have X without Y."* Find your version of "do you want fries with that?"
- **Menu** — **unsell** what they don't need → **prescribe** what they do, in personalized detail → **A/B** their preference → **card on file.** Never ask a yes/no question.
- **Anchor** — present the expensive thing first, get the gasp, supply a face-saving reason, descend, sell accessories last.
- **Rollover** — credit past spending (yours *or a competitor's*) toward a next offer priced **≥4× the credit**. Do this **before** issuing a refund.

### The Three Downsells
Any offer after a no is a downsell. Change **how they pay** or **what they get** — **never the same thing for less.**
- **Payment plan** — same product, split. Ask what they *can* do.
- **Trial with penalty** — the mirror of win-your-money-back: they only pay **if they don't meet the terms**. Offer last → **always get a card** → sell staying and paying → **explain fees after the card** → make check-ins required. *Just call it a free trial.*
- **Feature downsell** — cut a feature to justify a lower price. **Guarantees are features** — customers discover their value in the removal.

### The Three Continuity Plays
Run continuity **last**, then immediately upsell prepaid blocks.
- **Continuity bonus** — advertise the **bonus**, not the membership. Anchor its value, then *"Do you want to know how you can get this for free?"*
- **Continuity discount** — free time for a commitment. Apply the discount **after the first one or two payments** (collects cash, validates the card). **Extend** the term, don't consume it.
- **Waived fee** — setup fee of **3–5× monthly**, waived for a term, payable if they break it. *"Customers will stay longer if leaving costs more than staying."*

### Continuity Operating Tweaks (highest value per word in the book)
- **Bill every four weeks, not monthly** — 13 cycles vs. 12 = **+8.3%/yr.** At 20% margins that's **+41% annual profit** for changing a few words.
- **Add a 3% processing fee** — five extra words. At a 10% margin business, **+30% profit.**
- **Trade the fee for a second payment method** — fixes expired cards and insufficient funds. Get ACH if you can.
- **Bulk prepaid**: "buy 5, get 1 free" — only **1 in 8** takers lifts 30-day profit ~50%.
- **Lifetime discount** set just past your average churn month.
- **Price cancellation at the discount received**, make cancelling easy, and always run the exit interview — it saves ~1/3.

### Operating Principles
- **"How will I make this work for my business?"** replaces "this won't work for my business."
- **Hard selling is for weak products.** If they don't want it, find someone who does.
- **If a customer asks for their money back, give it back.** Don't take blood money.
- **Don't lie.** "Change reality to make the facts compelling, or learn to frame them in a way that is." You can't file bankruptcy on a reputation.
- **Obey advertising law** — especially around the word *free*, and especially with giveaways.
